# ملاحظات المراجعة — أوربت (Orbit Systems Monitor)

## App Review notes (EN) — also pasted into App Store Connect

Orbit is a native iOS shell (Capacitor) around our production service https://orbitq8.web.app. It is a monitoring dashboard that checks the health of the owner's own websites and code repositories and sends alerts. The real service is protected by a private operator passkey, so there is NO account to sign up for and nothing to buy.

HOW TO REVIEW (no account needed):
1. Open the app. On the login screen, in the field «رمز الدخول» (access code), type this code exactly: qg4qv2ggpbxxqb
2. Tap «دخول». The app opens an isolated DEMO with synthetic data only (a banner says «وضع تجريبي»). Nothing real is reachable from it.
3. Try: the platform list and status cards, tap a platform for its details and incident explanation, tap «تحديث» (refresh), and add a sample platform with a name. Tap «خروج» to leave the demo.

No account and no registration: operator access is by a private passkey; there is no self-service account creation, so there is no account-deletion flow to review. The demo stores nothing and is erased automatically after 30 minutes.

Native features: offline detection with banner and automatic recovery, pull-to-refresh, swipe-back navigation, resume at the last page, iPad multitasking and Apple-silicon Mac support.
Payments: none. Third-party analytics/ads: none. Privacy policy: https://orbitq8.web.app/privacy-policy.html
